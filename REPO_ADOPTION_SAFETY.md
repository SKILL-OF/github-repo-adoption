# Repo Adoption Safety

**Five patterns for safely adopting existing repos:**

## 1. Ownership Verification
Verify you have explicit permission to adopt (from prior owner or org admin). Never assume.

## 2. State Audit
Before adoption: read last 10 commits, check branches, verify no uncommitted work.

## 3. Clear Handoff
Document: who owned it, why they're passing it, what state it's in. Record timestamp.

## 4. Atomic Adoption
Create adoption record: timestamp, from-owner, to-owner, state-snapshot, reason.

## 5. Restart Safe
After restart, adoption record lets any agent verify: "This repo was adopted, here's the proof."
